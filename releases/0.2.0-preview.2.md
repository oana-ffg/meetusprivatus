# Transcription model choices and meeting deletion

Choose between Whisper tiny.en, base.en, small.en, and Parakeet TDT 0.6 v3 in Settings. Models run locally on the CPU. Download optional models with progress, stop and resume a download, and delete unused downloads after confirmation. Downloads pause when recording starts.

**First launch after this update asks you to choose a transcription model.** Nothing is selected automatically. Press **Choose model…**, select a model, then **Save** before recording. Whisper base.en is included and works offline without a download. Your saved choice is kept on later launches.

Parakeet v3 was faster and more accurate than the Whisper options on the public read-speech samples tested. It uses about 1 GB of memory and detects language automatically. Whisper small.en could not keep up with continuous speech on the development desktop. These measurements do not establish real-meeting accuracy or laptop performance.

Right-click a saved meeting and choose **Delete meeting…**, then confirm. Deletion is permanent. Files you added to a meeting folder are preserved. Active recordings cannot be deleted in normal single-state use.

The app and recording worker stay offline. Only pressing a download or update button starts a separate network helper, which reads no meeting content. ONNX Runtime is pinned to 1.28.0 to avoid the network uploader documented in later versions.

This is a preview. Real Zoom/Teams calls, low-powered-laptop performance, Irish and Danish accents, and full-worker network/crash/retention acceptance remain unverified. Separate development instances using different `--state-dir` values must not share a meeting destination: their recording/deletion/summary locks are not shared.

Update through **Check for updates → Restart to update**, or download and extract **MeetusPrivatus-win-x64-Portable.zip**. Meetings, settings, and downloaded models survive the in-app update. Windows 11 x64 and the Microsoft Visual C++ x64 runtime are required; releases are unsigned.
